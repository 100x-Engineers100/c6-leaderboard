Leaderboard – Mentee Scoring System (SOP)
1. Purpose
This SOP defines the standardized process for awarding and tracking leaderboard points for mentees. The objective is to ensure fairness, transparency, and consistency in evaluating participation, submissions, and overall engagement throughout the cohort.
2. Scope
This SOP applies to all mentees enrolled in the program and covers all activities that contribute to leaderboard points during the cohort duration.
3. Responsibility
Program / Operations Team
* Track activities and submissions
* Validate participation and posts
* Update the leaderboard regularly
Mentors / Evaluators
* Review assignments, hackathons, and capstone projects
* Provide scores based on evaluation criteria
Mentees
* Attend sessions and participate actively
* Submit assignments and projects within deadlines
* Ensure authenticity and accuracy in submissions
________________


4. Scoring Components
Leaderboard points are awarded across the following activities:
4.1 Attendance
Attendance is tracked for live lectures conducted on Friday and Saturday.
Scoring Rule
* 20 points per lecture attended
________________


4.2 Assignments
Assignments are evaluated and scored out of 20 marks.
Scoring Rule
* Leaderboard points will be equal to the assignment score
* Example:
   * Score 16/20 → 16 leaderboard points
   * Score 20/20 → 20 leaderboard points
Assignments must be submitted within the defined deadline to receive points.
________________


4.3 #0to100xEngineer Challenge
Mentees are encouraged to share their learning journey publicly as part of the Build in Public challenge.
Scoring Rule
* 20 points per valid post
* Additional 10 bonus points awarded at the end of the week for 7 consecutive days of posting
Posts must:
* Be original
* Reflect progress or learning related to the program
* Be publicly accessible
________________


4.4 Mini Hackathon
The mini hackathon is evaluated out of 100 marks.
Scoring Rule
* Leaderboard points will be equal to the hackathon score
* Example:
   * 92/100 → 92 leaderboard points
________________


4.5 Mid Capstone Project
The mid capstone milestone evaluates early project progress.
Scoring Rule
* 50 points awarded upon submission
Points are granted for successful submission within the defined deadline.
________________


4.6 Final Capstone Project
The final capstone project is the largest scoring component of the leaderboard.
Scoring Rule
* 200 points awarded upon submission
* The final capstone carries the highest weightage on the leaderboard and provides mentees an opportunity to recover points they may have missed earlier and improve their overall ranking.
This reflects the completion and delivery of the final project.
________________


5. Validation & Exceptions
* Any discrepancy in leaderboard points must be reported within 48 hours of leaderboard updates.
* The Program Team’s decision will be final regarding validations or disputes.
________________


6. Leaderboard Updates
Leaderboard scores will be updated periodically (weekly) by the program team.
Mentees are encouraged to track their progress and standings regularly.
